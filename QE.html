QUALITY EDUCATION

PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College (COC) was founded in 1948 as the Parent-Teacher College — a non-political and non-sectarian institution dedicated to delivering quality and affordable education. It initially catered to both the youth and working individuals by offering primary to tertiary-level programs.

Over the decades, PHINMA COC expanded its curriculum to include programs in Computer Science, Engineering, and Mass Communications. In 2005, the college became part of the PHINMA Education Network, a milestone that marked a period of rapid growth and academic innovation.

Guided by PHINMA’s vision of nation-building through education, COC adopted non-traditional, student-focused teaching methods that equip learners with real-world skills. With industry-relevant programs and values-driven instruction, PHINMA COC prepares graduates not just for jobs—but for life.

  • Accessible and affordable education for all
  • Career-oriented curriculum with hands-on training
  • Experienced faculty and modern learning facilities
  • Supportive environment for holistic student growth

At PHINMA COC, we don’t just educate — we empower lives.
